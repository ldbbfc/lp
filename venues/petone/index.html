Welcome to Our Petone Venue

Our flagship venue at McEwan Park offers an exceptional Little Dribblers experience with beautiful waterfront location, excellent facilities, and a thriving community of families.

📍 Location & Facilities

McEwan Park is perfectly situated along Marine Parade in Petone, offering families a scenic and convenient location for Saturday morning football sessions.

  • Address: McEwan Park, Marine Parade, Petone
  • Parking: Ample free street parking along Marine Parade and nearby streets
  • Public Transport: Petone train station is just a 5-minute walk away
  • Facilities: Clean public toilets, playground for siblings, nearby cafés

⏰ Session Schedule

Saturday Morning Sessions

All sessions run for 30 minutes with professional referees and a supportive community atmosphere.

  • Very Little (0-2 years): 9:30am start - FREE for first term!
  • Little (2-3 years): 10:00am start - from $85 per 10-week term
  • Middle (4-5 years): 10:30am start - from $85 per 10-week term
  • Big (6-7 years): 11:00am start - from $85 per 10-week term

🚗 Getting There

From Wellington CBD: Take State Highway 2 north for 10 minutes. Exit at Petone and follow Marine Parade to McEwan Park.

From Upper Hutt: Take State Highway 2 south for 15 minutes. Exit at Petone.

Public Transport: Petone train station is a 5-minute walk from the venue.

🅿️ Parking & Facilities

  • Free street parking available along Marine Parade
  • Additional parking on side streets (Jackson Street, Cuba Street)
  • Public toilets available at the park
  • Playground for siblings to enjoy before/after sessions
  • Café nearby for post-game treats
