What to Expect

Everything you need to know for game day success

📦 What to Bring

  • Your little dribbler in their team kit
  • Comfortable shoes (boots available at venue)
  • Shin pads (highly recommended!)
  • Drink and snack for halftime
  • A big smile for encouragement

⏰ On Game Day

  • Arrive 10-20 mins before kickoff
  • Check field assignment at the green vehicle
  • Games last 30 minutes (2 x 12-15 min halves)
  • Referee manages the game
  • No coaches needed - just fun!

🌧️ Weather Cancellations

  • Decision made by 9am game day
  • Check Facebook or your email
  • 8+ sessions = full term
  • Credit offered if under 8 sessions

👪 For Parents

  • Join them on field to start
  • Step back once they're comfortable
  • Best place to start: in the goal!
  • Support all players and the ref
  • No dogs please!

Remember: Participation is encouraged, never forced!

Standing on the field is a win! They'll join in when they're ready. Every child develops at their own pace, and fun is our main objective.
